Reshaping Palestinian Higher Education Governance under Conditions of Instability

An Analytical Study of Institutional Resilience in Universities Facing Compound Crises

Research type: Thesis research / Master's research
Status: In progress
Context: Palestine · Higher education

Research question

How can Palestinian higher education institutions reshape governance practices and strengthen institutional resilience in the face of compound crises?

Methodology

A qualitative descriptive-analytical approach based on document analysis of strategic plans and policy reports, supported by guided interviews with a purposive sample of academic and administrative leaders, subject to the final approved study design.

Key themes

Institutional governanceHigher educationInstitutional resilienceCompound crises

Abstract / Summary

This study examines the governance challenges facing Palestinian universities under conditions of instability, focusing on adaptation, response capacity, and continuity. This summary should be updated when the final research version and findings are formally approved.

Keywords: Governance, higher education, Palestine, resilience, sustainability

About

About Me

Shatha Dweikat, a social impact researcher, combining an academic background in social work with a practical orientation toward corporate social responsibility and sustainable development.

My Journey

My journey began with understanding people and their needs through studying social work, and from there my interest expanded toward development, community, and institutional responsibility issues. I seek to deepen my knowledge of social and developmental issues by combining an academic foundation, continuous learning, and community practices.

I believe that investing in human potential is the foundation for building stronger communities and a more sustainable future, and I work to translate this belief into knowledge content and training programs that empower individuals and organizations to adopt sustainable, effective practices.

Vision

Building knowledge and human spaces that help people discover their potential, understand themselves, and develop their abilities — becoming active partners in creating positive, sustainable change in their communities.

Mission

We create spaces for learning, growth, and knowledge exchange that empower individuals to discover their potential, develop their abilities, and turn knowledge and awareness into practical steps toward a more conscious and sustainable society.

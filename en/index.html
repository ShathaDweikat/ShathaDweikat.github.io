From Social Work to Research & Impact

I am a licensed Social Worker and researcher focused on empowerment, social impact, and sustainable development. My path combines an academic foundation in Social Work with a Master's degree in Corporate Social Responsibility and Sustainable Development, along with a continuous commitment to research, learning, and designing knowledge programs and content.

I focus on understanding the relationship between people, institutions, and society — exploring how knowledge can be turned into interventions and practices that are more conscious and sustainable.

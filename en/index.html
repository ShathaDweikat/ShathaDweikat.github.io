About

My Journey & Vision

An academic and professional background that brings together people, knowledge, and sustainable development.

Building Knowledge for Purposeful Impact

I am a licensed social specialist and a researcher in empowerment and social impact, combining an academic foundation in Social Work with a completed Master's degree in Corporate Social Responsibility and Sustainable Development. I seek to deepen my understanding of social and developmental issues through an academic foundation, continuous learning, and community practices.

I work to translate this orientation into knowledge content and training programs that empower individuals and organizations to adopt sustainable, effective practices — turning theoretical knowledge into practical steps with tangible impact.

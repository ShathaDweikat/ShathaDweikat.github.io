What Is Journey of Change?

Journey of Change is an educational space that helps you understand yourself, become aware of the factors influencing change, and turn the desire for growth into practical, applicable steps.

Course Topics

Understanding the concept and stages of change
Building self-awareness and understanding personal patterns
Navigating challenges and obstacles
Building practical steps toward development and growth

# Content Archive Guide

## Research Project record order

Every new research record should use this order:

1. Title
2. Research type: Academic paper, Research project, Literature review, Policy analysis, or Thesis research
3. Status: Completed, In progress, or Under development
4. Research question
5. Methodology
6. Key themes
7. Abstract / Summary
8. PDF or external link
9. Keywords

The Arabic and English research pages currently contain one structured research record and a placeholder for future records. Duplicate the `archive-card` structure in the relevant language page, then replace the content without changing the field order.

## Articles & Analysis

Each original article should have its own page under `articles/` and `en/articles/`. Use the corresponding `article-template.html` as the starting point. Each article contains:

- Title
- Date
- Category
- Short abstract
- Full article
- LinkedIn discussion link

## Knowledge Library record order

Each resource should be added under the appropriate category using this order:

1. Title
2. Organization/Author
3. Year
4. Topic
5. 3–5 line summary
6. Original link

The library categories are Institutional Sources, Academic Articles, Policy Reports, Research Guides, and Books. Institutional sources include UN Reports, World Bank, UN Women, UNDP, and OECD resources.

The Do No Harm Framework: A Comprehensive Guide to Conflict-Sensitive Humanitarian Aid

Original organization: Collaborative for Development Action (CDA)
Topic: Conflict-sensitive humanitarian aid

Summary

The Do No Harm Framework is a conflict-sensitivity tool developed to help humanitarian organizations understand how aid interacts with the social, political, and economic dynamics of communities affected by conflict. This guide presents the framework’s foundation, practical tools, and six universal lessons for reducing unintended harm while strengthening the positive impact of humanitarian interventions.

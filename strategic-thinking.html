Learning Program

Fundamentals of Strategic Thinking

A practical learning program focused on vision, goal setting, strategic thinking, and action.

Overview

This program introduces the foundations of strategic thinking and helps participants connect a clear vision with realistic goals and practical action.

What it covers

Strategic thinking, vision, goal setting, prioritization, decision-making, and translating ideas into action.

Status

Under development. The program will be made available when its learning materials are ready.

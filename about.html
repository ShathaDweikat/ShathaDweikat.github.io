About Me

About Me

Shatha Dweikat — a licensed social worker and researcher in empowerment and social impact. My journey started at the closest point to people: social work, and from there my focus expanded toward corporate social responsibility and sustainable development.

Licensed Social Worker (PUSWP) CSR & Sustainable Development Researcher Empowerment & Social Impact

From Social Work to Social Impact

I am a licensed social worker and researcher with a background in Social Work and Family Studies and postgraduate studies in Corporate Social Responsibility and Sustainable Development.

My interests sit at the intersection of social empowerment, sustainable development, CSR, community development, and social impact. I explore how individuals, communities, and institutions can build capacity, strengthen resilience, and contribute to meaningful and sustainable change.

Alongside research, I develop educational content and learning programs that connect knowledge with practical growth and capacity building.

I believe that sustainable change begins with understanding people and their contexts — and that knowledge becomes meaningful when it leads to learning, action, and impact.

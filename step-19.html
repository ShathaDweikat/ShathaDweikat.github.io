Community Initiative

Step 19

A proposed transition initiative supporting orphaned girls aged 17–19 as they prepare for independent life.

Purpose

Step 19 supports orphaned girls aged 17–19 during the transition toward independent life through psychosocial preparation, individual planning, safe transitional housing, economic empowerment, and continued follow-up.

Core components

Psychosocial support, individual transition plans, safe transitional housing, education and employment pathways, economic empowerment, and monitoring and evaluation.

Partnership approach

The initiative is designed around coordination between social protection, education, employment, economic empowerment, care homes, psychosocial support providers, civil society, and private-sector partners.
